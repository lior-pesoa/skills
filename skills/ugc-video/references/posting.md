# Posting notes

Write these into a text file next to the render (`<NAME>-CAPTIONS.txt`), plain text, ready to paste. Last checked 2026-10-21 on Instagram.

## What goes in the file

1. **File and length** of the render to upload.
2. **Captions:** "use Edits auto captions, then fix <words it gets wrong>", where to place them (right side, about 50 to 75% down, off the avatar's face), and the timed lines from `caption-groups.py` for typing by hand.
3. **Optional hook headline** for the first 3 seconds, top of the screen.
4. **Post caption:**
   ```
   Line 1: the keyword hook in the words the audience searches (trade + app).
   1 to 2 sentences: what it means for them, plainly.
   CTA: "Free check, link in bio." (links in captions don't work)
   3 to 5 hashtags, niche first.
   ```
   It must open differently from any other reel on the same topic.
5. **AI disclosure:** turn on the AI label (Advanced settings) for a realistic AI person or voice. If the account has no toggle, add a caption line before the hashtags: "<Name> is an AI-generated presenter."
6. **Alt text:** often not offered for reels. Look under Advanced settings > Accessibility, or after posting under ⋯ > Edit. If it isn't there, skip it.
7. **Music:** a track from the platform's ad-licensed library (Meta Sound Collection on Instagram), very low under the voice. Never a trending track on anything that will be boosted.
8. **Cover:** a designed frame (the end card), uploaded from the camera roll.
9. **Ad link** with UTM tags, one campaign name per format (`<trade>-ugc`), so it can be compared with other formats.

## Two reels on one topic

Fine, when they are different videos (different hook and story, e.g. a screen-story reel and a UGC dare):
- Post 2 to 4 days apart.
- Different hook, caption opening and cover.
- Think about the grid: an AI face next to real photo posts can look odd; "Also share to feed" off keeps it in the Reels tab.
- Boost as two ads, same audience and budget, and compare by UTM campaign.
